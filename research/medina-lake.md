## Housing Stock
Recreational and weekend-home community surrounding Medina Lake, roughly 35 miles northwest of SA. Mix of 1970s-90s fishing cabins and newer lakefront homes. Many are second properties — owners visit seasonally and need seasonal cleanup services rather than recurring weekly mowing.

## Common Problems
1. Seasonal overgrowth during extended owner absence
2. Post-storm debris from Hill Country weather events
3. Brush and cedar encroachment on steep lakeside terrain
4. Vacation property preparation before selling or renting
5. Junk and debris left behind by seasonal renters

## Content Angles
1. Seasonal property prep — owners want their lake house ready to enjoy, not to work on
2. Pre-sale cleanup — Medina Lake properties sell for premium when they show well
3. Storm response — remote location means owners need a trusted crew who can handle issues without supervision
4. Absentee property management — reliable service for owners who can't be there every week
5. Steep terrain mowing — lakefront lots are often challenging grades requiring specialized equipment
