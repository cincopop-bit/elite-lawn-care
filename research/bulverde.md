## Housing Stock
Rapidly growing suburb north of SA in Comal County, primarily 2010s-2020s construction. Master-planned communities like Spring Branch Crossing and Johnson Ranch mix with older 1-3 acre rural properties. Large lot sizes and heavy Hill Country tree canopy. One of the fastest-growing communities in Texas.

## Common Problems
1. Cedar and live oak management on large lots
2. New construction debris and landscaping establishment on fresh builds
3. HOA compliance in newer master-planned sections
4. Steep terrain and rocky limestone outcroppings challenge standard equipment
5. Rapid population growth means lawn care providers are often overbooked
