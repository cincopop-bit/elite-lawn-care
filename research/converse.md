## Housing Stock
Established suburb on SA's northeast side, primarily 1960s-1990s construction. Dense residential neighborhoods of modest brick homes on 6,000-9,000 sq ft lots. Adjacent to Randolph AFB — strong military family presence. Older tree canopy with mature live oaks, cedar elms, and pecans throughout.

## Common Problems
1. Mature tree limb drop and overhang over structures
2. Dense neighborhood means front-yard curb appeal is visible and matters
3. Military family turnover — properties need rapid cleanup between occupants
4. Older lots with overgrown foundation plantings that need heavy cutting back
5. Junk removal during PCS moves (permanent change of station) is a specific recurring need
