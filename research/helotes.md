## Housing Stock
Hill Country suburb northwest of SA on the Bandera Road corridor. Mix of 1970s-90s ranch homes and newer 2000s-2020s builds on large lots of 1-3 acres. Low-density, semi-rural feel despite proximity to Loop 1604. Many homeowners have horses or chickens — wide perimeter and pasture areas needing management.

## Common Problems
1. Ashe juniper (cedar) encroachment on back acreage and fence lines
2. Large live oak limb risk — heavy canopy over structures
3. Brush accumulation creating fire risk in dry months
4. Uneven terrain from limestone outcroppings challenges equipment
5. Post-storm debris from Helotes Creek corridor

## Local Texture
John T. Floore Country Store (historic landmark), Helotes Creek, Helotes Civic Center. Strong community identity — word of mouth is the primary referral channel here. Tight-knit homeowners who stay loyal to reliable service providers.

## Content Angles
1. Cedar management — Helotes homeowners battle cedar year-round and need a crew who understands selective vs. full clearing
2. Large lot scheduling — acreage properties need planned service visits, not one-off calls
3. Brush fire prevention — seasonal clearance of dry brush is a real safety concern
4. Helotes Creek storm cleanup — flooding and debris events affect creek-adjacent properties
5. Rural property pride — Helotes residents take property appearance seriously despite the rural setting
