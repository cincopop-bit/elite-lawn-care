## Housing Stock
Gated and semi-gated Hill Country community near Boerne, primarily 1990s-2010s custom homes on 1-3 acre wooded lots. Heavy live oak and Ashe juniper canopy. Stone and cedar architectural character. Upper-middle to affluent demographic. Managed by a community association with appearance standards.

## Common Problems
1. Dense cedar canopy — cedar encroachment is aggressive and constant on wooded lots
2. Live oak canopy management — large crowns over rooflines require professional handling
3. Rocky Hill Country terrain challenges standard mowing equipment
4. Community association appearance standards must be met
5. Seasonal brush accumulation and fire risk on uncleared back lot portions

## Content Angles
1. Hill Country wooded lot expertise — Timberwood Park's terrain and tree types require specific knowledge and equipment
2. Cedar clearing without destroying native character — selective clearing preserves the Hill Country aesthetic residents love
3. HOA compliance on wooded lots — meeting appearance standards when the whole property is essentially a forest
4. Fire risk management — defensible space matters when homes are surrounded by dense cedar
5. Premium rural community expectations — residents chose Timberwood Park for the environment; Elite enhances it without disrupting it
