## Housing Stock
Upscale equestrian community on the northwest corridor straddling Bexar and Kendall counties. Estate-style homes on 2-10 acre horse properties. HOA-governed with strict curb appeal standards. Mix of 1990s custom builds and newer luxury estates. Very high property values.

## Common Problems
1. High HOA standards require consistent, professional-grade maintenance
2. Large estate lots with multiple zones: lawn, pasture, ornamental beds, tree canopy
3. Formal landscaping elements (stone borders, irrigation beds) require careful trimming around
4. Tree health management on mature live oaks and pecan trees
5. Seasonal leaf and debris cleanup before real estate showings

## Local Texture
Equestrian community atmosphere. One of the highest-value residential communities in the SA metro. Residents expect reliability, punctuality, and premium results. Referrals and reputation matter enormously here.

## Content Angles
1. Estate lawn care — large properties need crews that can handle multi-zone maintenance in one visit
2. Pre-listing cleanup — Fair Oaks Ranch real estate moves fast when properties look pristine
3. Formal bed maintenance — ornamental and native plantings require experienced trimming, not just mowing
4. Tree health and liability — mature oaks over structures need regular assessment and crown cleaning
5. Same-day availability — affluent homeowners value responsiveness above all else
