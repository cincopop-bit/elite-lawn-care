## Housing Stock
Mid-size incorporated suburb on SA's northwest side. Primarily 1950s-1980s brick ranch homes on 7,000-12,000 sq ft lots. Established neighborhood character with mature trees. Working-to-middle-class homeowner demographics. Leon Creek runs through the area.

## Common Problems
1. Leon Creek corridor — flood debris and overgrowth along the creek easement
2. Mature tree management — 40-60 year old trees need crown cleaning and limb removal
3. Established foundation plants overgrown and in need of major cutting back
4. HOA and city code compliance on street-facing yards
5. Junk accumulation in older garages and sheds — removal before home sales
