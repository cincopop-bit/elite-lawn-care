## Housing Stock
Fast-growing I-35 corridor city between SA and Austin. Mix of older historic German-heritage homes near downtown and massive new master-planned development (Veramendi, Vintage Oaks, Creekside). Extremely diverse lot sizes — small urban lots near the Comal River to large acreage in new suburban zones.

## Common Problems
1. Comal and Guadalupe River corridor storm and flood cleanup
2. New development landscaping establishment on bare lots
3. Turf establishment in sandy loam and limestone soils
4. Rapid influx of new residents unfamiliar with Texas-specific lawn care needs
5. River rock and natural stone integration with lawn maintenance

## Content Angles
1. River community — flood and storm cleanup after Comal/Guadalupe events is a specific, high-urgency service
2. New resident education — Austin and DFW transplants don't know Texas grasses; Elite guides the transition
3. Tourism-adjacent property care — short-term rentals near the river need consistent presentation
4. German heritage neighborhood maintenance — older downtown tree canopy requires professional care
5. I-35 growth corridor — NB's explosive growth means ongoing demand for new-home landscaping services
