## Housing Stock
Small incorporated city entirely surrounded by San Antonio. One of the smallest cities in the SA metro (about 8,000 residents). Primarily 1950s-1970s working-class homes on small to mid-size lots. Dense urban feel. Adjacent to Fort Sam Houston.

## Common Problems
1. Small lot, high-density neighborhoods with aging foundation plantings
2. Junk accumulation in yards on small urban lots
3. Overgrown back yards that have been deferred due to cost or time
4. Debris and haul-off after DIY projects in older homes
5. Tree limb removal in tight spaces with little clearance

## Content Angles
1. Small city, big service — Kirby residents often get overlooked by larger lawn companies
2. Junk removal in tight spaces — small lots mean creative access and careful loading
3. Affordable cleanup — working-class community values fair pricing and honest work
4. Quick in-and-out service — small lots mean faster jobs and faster scheduling
5. Neighbor referrals — dense community means one satisfied customer leads to five calls on the same block
