## Housing Stock
Semi-rural unincorporated community along I-10 Northwest corridor. Mix of older rural properties, ranches, and newer upscale custom homes taking advantage of the scenic Hill Country landscape. Lot sizes typically 2-10 acres. Limestone terrain and heavy cedar/live oak cover.

## Common Problems
1. Heavy cedar encroachment — one of the densest cedar areas in the SA metro
2. Large acreage maintenance requires multiple-person crews
3. Steep terrain and limestone outcroppings challenge equipment
4. Brush accumulation and fire risk on unmanaged back acreage
5. Drainage easements and TxDOT-adjacent right-of-way maintenance

## Content Angles
1. I-10 corridor cedar — Leon Springs sits right in the cedar belt; clearance is a year-round reality
2. Acreage specialists — Elite can handle 5-acre properties with the right equipment and crew size
3. Rural ranch service — clients here need more than a lawn guy; they need a land management crew
4. Fire preparedness — brush clearance around structures is a property protection issue, not just aesthetics
5. Privacy and discretion — semi-rural clients value a crew that works independently without supervision
