## Housing Stock
One of SA's largest master-planned communities in the 78253 zip code, northwest SA near Culebra Road. Built primarily 2004-2022. Dense HOA-managed neighborhoods with Bermuda and St. Augustine lawns. Standard lots of 6,000-10,000 sq ft. Very young tree canopy — most trees under 20 years old. High density of young families.

## Common Problems
1. HOA compliance violations for unmowed or edged lawns — notices issued frequently
2. St. Augustine patch disease and chinch bug pressure during summer heat
3. Bermuda runners invading flowerbeds and sidewalk cracks
4. Young trees needing formative pruning before they become structural problems
5. Post-construction debris from ongoing neighborhood development nearby

## Local Texture
Near major retail (Walmart, HEB), top-rated Northside ISD schools. Fastest-growing quadrant of SA. Young professional families with dual incomes — they want reliable, recurring service they don't have to think about.

## Content Angles
1. HOA compliance scheduling — Elite keeps Alamo Ranch lawns violation-free year-round
2. Young lawn establishment — newer sod needs different care than mature grass
3. Recurring service plans — busy families in Alamo Ranch want a set-it-and-forget-it solution
4. Fast turnaround — dense neighborhood means neighbors notice, and referrals travel fast
5. Summer drought management — keeping St. Augustine alive through July and August is a known challenge here
