## Housing Stock
Small affluent enclave of about 3,000 residents entirely surrounded by North San Antonio. Primarily 1960s-1990s custom homes on larger-than-average lots (10,000-20,000 sq ft) with established mature trees. Very high income demographic. One of the most well-maintained residential communities in the SA metro.

## Common Problems
1. High presentation standards — one unmaintained yard is an anomaly and draws immediate attention
2. Mature tree management — established pecan, live oak, and cedar elm require professional-grade care
3. Formal landscape bed maintenance — residents maintain investment-level ornamental plantings
4. Seasonal cleanup timing matters — residents coordinate with neighbors on leaf and debris management
5. Reliable scheduling is non-negotiable — homeowners here have zero tolerance for missed appointments

## Content Angles
1. Community standards — Hollywood Park has an unofficial expectation of perfection; Elite delivers it
2. Mature tree canopy — valuable trees over 50 years old need expert hands, not just a chainsaw
3. Premium client service — punctuality, communication, and clean work are the price of entry here
4. Real estate value — landscaping in Hollywood Park directly correlates to sale price and time on market
5. Neighbor referral culture — one excellent job leads to multiple homes on the same street
