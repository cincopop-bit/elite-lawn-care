## Housing Stock
Small historic Alsatian settlement 20 miles west of SA along US-90. Agricultural and semi-rural character. Older homes on large lots with mature pecan, mesquite, and oak trees. Farming operations adjacent to residential areas. Lower density, loyal customer base that sticks with reliable local providers.

## Common Problems
1. Large lot mowing on uneven rural terrain
2. Agricultural debris and green waste haul-off after clearing
3. Pecan and mesquite tree trimming — both drop significant debris seasonally
4. Medina River flooding events cause debris and erosion cleanup needs
5. Rural lot encroachment — cedar and native brush reclaim open ground quickly if unmanaged

## Local Texture
Medina River runs through town. Historic Alsatian architecture district. Small-town character — personal relationships and reliability are the only marketing that works here. Seasonal flooding in low-lying river areas is a recurring annual issue.

## Content Angles
1. Rural lot clearing — Castroville landowners need crews who can handle large unmanaged acreage, not just suburban lawns
2. Medina River storm cleanup — debris removal after flood events is a specific, recurring need
3. Pecan season — massive leaf and nut drop in fall requires dedicated cleanup
4. Agricultural-adjacent services — haul-off and brush clearing that most lawn companies won't touch
5. Loyalty market — reliable, professional service stands out dramatically in a small town with few quality options
