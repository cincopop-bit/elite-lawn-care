## Housing Stock
Prestigious inner-loop enclave surrounded by SA. Primarily 1920s-1960s construction — a mix of Tudor revivals, Spanish colonials, and classic ranch-styles on generous lots. Mature, established tree canopy including enormous live oaks, pecan trees, and magnolias. One of the wealthiest zip codes in SA (78209).

## Common Problems
1. Massive live oak canopy management — trees over 100 years old require careful crown cleaning
2. Formal landscape beds with high-standard trimming expectations
3. Pre-listing curb appeal preparation for high-value real estate sales
4. Storm and winter freeze damage on irreplaceable mature trees
5. Invasive ligustrum and Japanese privet encroachment in older gardens

## Content Angles
1. Historic tree care — Alamo Heights' live oaks are irreplaceable assets; residents want crews who handle them respectfully
2. White-glove presentation — AH homeowners expect precision edging, clean beds, zero mess left behind
3. Pre-sale preparation — homes here sell for $800K-$2M+; curb appeal investment pays enormous dividends
